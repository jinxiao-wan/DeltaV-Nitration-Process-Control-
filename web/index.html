    02 / THE ORIGINAL DESIGN

    Three layers of engineering.

    The thesis connects system architecture, control logic and operator interaction.

    01 / HARDWARE

    A distributed architecture

    Three controller areas, an engineering station and eight operator stations. A combination of fieldbus and conventional I/O connects the field devices.

    02 / CONTROL

    Cascade with a ratio follower

    A temperature master passes a flow setpoint to a faster inner loop. The second feed follows the measured first-feed flow through a ratio reference.

    03 / OPERATION

    Make the process visible

    Explorer defines the system; Control Studio links the function blocks; Live/Operate screens present process values, alarms and historical trends.

    An unresolved I/O count in the thesis

    The body reports 49 AI + 4 AO + 5 DI + 10 DO = 68 points. Table 3.1 lists 42 AI + 30 AO + 3 DI + 3 DO = 78 points for area 301. Both records are retained; neither is presented as a verified final inventory.

    03 / DEVELOPMENT PROCESS

    From requirements to configuration.

    The documented 2024 workflow, followed by the 2026 reconstruction.

    01

    Understand the process

    Review the nitration unit, its inputs and outputs, and the variables that control temperature and feed balance.

    02

    Define the DCS

    Design stations, controller areas and the fieldbus / I/O connections. Identify signal and hardware requirements.

    03

    Build the loops

    Develop single, cascade and ratio diagrams. Link input, controller, output and tracking function blocks.

    04

    Sequence & observe

    Configure startup steps and alarm parameters, build HMI pages, then inspect simulated historical trends.

    What was recovered?

    The archive supplies thesis and defence evidence, including 16 figures used here. It does not include a native DeltaV project database, FHX export, raw historian data or executable model. The defence identifies the original work as theoretical/simulation-level research. This new control lab demonstrates the concepts without claiming to reproduce the original runtime.
